Welcome to Emergency Dentistry

Urgent care when you need it the most

At Dental Specialists Turramurra, we understand that dental emergencies can happen at any time, causing discomfort and anxiety. That's why we offer emergency dentistry services to address urgent dental concerns promptly and efficiently. Whether you're experiencing severe pain, have suffered a dental injury, or are facing another immediate dental issue, our team is prepared to provide the care you need when you need it the most.

Our Services

Our Emergency Dentistry Services

Emergency dental situations require swift action to alleviate pain, prevent further damage, and ensure a positive outcome. Our emergency services include, but are not limited to:

Pain Relief

Pain Relief

Treatment for severe toothaches, abscesses, or pain caused by decay or injury.

Injury

Injury Treatment

Immediate care for dental injuries, including knocked-out teeth, broken or chipped teeth, and injuries to the gums or jaw.

Infection

Infection Control

Prompt treatment for infections, including abscesses, to prevent the spread of infection and alleviate severe pain.
